About

What five years of shipping React Native apps has actually taught me.

I started building mobile apps back when React Native still felt like an experiment. Five years and a couple dozen releases later, it's the tool I trust most for getting a product from idea to both app stores without doubling the team.

I care most about the parts users never think about: launch time, list scrolling that doesn't stutter, and a build pipeline that doesn't break the week before a release. I've worked solo on early-stage apps and inside larger teams shipping to millions of users, and I like the handoff between those two worlds — moving fast without leaving a mess for whoever scales it next.

Outside of React Native, I dip into native Swift and Kotlin when a feature genuinely needs it, and I've picked up enough backend and DevOps to not be helpless without one.
